Who We Are
About Shinzu Family
A haven for cat enthusiasts in the heart of Dhaka.
Welcome to Shinzu Family — House of Ragdoll, Persian, and Scottish Fold, a haven for cat enthusiasts. We proudly operate from our address at House-4, Road-1, Block-B, Chandrima Model Town, Dhaka Uddan in Dhaka, Bangladesh, and delight in ensuring your furry felines receive only the best we can offer.

Our services cater to Mohammadpur, Dhaka, Bangladesh, and we take pride in providing high-quality Persian, Ragdoll, Scottish Fold, and British Shorthair cats at reasonable prices. From finding your ideal Persian cat companion to grooming and nutrition tips, and recently we have added world-class cat foods and essentials.

Shinzu Family — House of Ragdoll, Persian, and Scottish Fold is here to cater to all your cat needs.
